# Business Purpose: Ruwi’s Cakes
## Core Mission
Ruwi’s Cakes exists to bridge the gap between high-end artisanal baking and digital convenience. The business’s primary purpose is to provide premium, trend-driven custom cakes through a frictionless online experience, removing the traditional "back-and-forth" stress of custom ordering.

## Key Value Propositions
Convenience-First Model: Designed to offer a "three-click" ordering process, enabling customers to secure professional-grade cakes for weddings, birthdays, and corporate events with minimal lead time (often 24–48 hours).

Artisanal Quality at Scale: Unlike mass-produced alternatives, every cake is handmade using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, maintaining the "hobbyist’s passion" from which the brand originated.

Inclusive Innovation: A commitment to dietary diversity, offering a wide range of vegan and gluten-friendly options without compromising on the "aesthetic-first" design philosophy.

Logistical Reliability: Operating a specialized delivery network across Melbourne to ensure that fragile, multi-tiered products arrive safely and on time, taking 100% responsibility for the "last mile" of the celebration.

Operational Context for Documentation
Founder: Ruwindi Serasinghe (started the business following a career-ending injury as a professional badminton player).

Primary Markets: B2C (Weddings, Birthdays, Milestones) and B2B (Corporate catering, branded cakes).

Key Tech/Process: Utilizes a 3D cake designing platform and an online-first storefront to streamline customization and logistics.

## Summary 
"Ruwi’s Cakes is an online-first premium bakery specializing in trend-responsive, handmade custom cakes. Its purpose is to modernize the custom cake industry by combining high-end craftsmanship with a frictionless digital ordering and delivery system, making luxury celebration cakes accessible, affordable, and stress-free."
